# HeinOnline usage permission (UTulsa MLIC library), received 2026-11-12

Summary of the librarian's reply (confirmed with HeinOnline):
- Paced downloads are OK for this project, including automated/scripted downloads.
- Allowed use: analysis; cite HeinOnline and GAO; do NOT redistribute full texts.
- Before the paper or software prototype includes or shares any full texts: check with the librarian first.

Project rules derived from this:
- Raw HeinOnline texts live only in data/heinonline_raw/ (git-ignored, never published or committed).
- Derived data (structured fields, outcomes, statistics) may be used in the paper/prototype.
- Downloads are paced (one document at a time, several seconds apart).

## Follow-up approval, 2026-11-12 (UTulsa library, by email)

After I disclosed that ~3,300 decisions had been collected (more than the 1,000–2,000 originally described), the library replied:

1. The ~3,300 decisions already collected are acceptable under the license for **private research use**.
2. Permission granted to collect the remaining decisions, **provided the same respectful pacing and privacy terms are maintained** (paced scripted downloads, no redistribution of full texts, raw texts kept private in git-ignored data/heinonline_raw/).

Still required: check with the librarian before any paper or software prototype includes or shares full texts. Commercial use is not covered by this approval.
